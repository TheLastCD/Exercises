Make automation is referred to as implicit rules

implicit rule variables:
	CC	c compiler	default cc
	CXX	c++ compiler	default G++

	Flags for the above compilers are designated using:
		CFLAGS given to CC
		CXXFLAGS given CXX
		CPPFLAGS is given to the C preprocessor (includes)
		LDDFLAGS given to compilers when they are supposed to invoke the linker
